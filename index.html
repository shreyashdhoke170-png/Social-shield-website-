BUILD BETTER ONLINE HABITS

Your everyday safety toolkit

01

Use strong passwords

Use long, unique passwords for different accounts. Consider a reputable password manager.

02

Turn on 2-step verification

Where available, enable multi-factor authentication to add another layer of account protection.

03

Review privacy settings

Check who can see your posts, contact you, tag you, and view personal details.

04

Pause before you post

Avoid sharing passwords, live location, identity documents, or private information publicly.

05

Check links and messages

Be cautious of urgent messages, suspicious links, unexpected attachments, and giveaway scams.

06

Be kind and report abuse

Think before commenting. Block and report harmful accounts, and ask a trusted person for help when needed.

KNOW WHAT YOU LEAVE BEHIND

What is a digital footprint?

A digital footprint is the trail of information connected to your activity online. It can shape how others understand your interests, behaviour, and reputation.

↗

Active footprint

Information you deliberately share, such as posts, comments, likes, photos, and profile details.

◎

Passive footprint

Information collected as you browse or use services, such as cookies, device details, or activity data.
