01 — SOCIAL MEDIA SAFETY

Small habits make a big difference.

Social media can be useful and fun, but safe digital habits help protect your identity, privacy, and accounts.

🔐

Strong passwords

Use long, unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid obvious information such as your name or birthday.

🛡️

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds another layer of protection if someone learns your password.

⚙️

Check privacy settings

Review who can see your posts, contact you, tag you, or access information connected to your account.

🔗

Think before clicking

Be cautious with unexpected links, urgent messages, giveaways, and requests for passwords or codes.

02 — DIGITAL FOOTPRINT

Your online actions can leave a lasting trail.

A digital footprint is the record of information and activity connected to your online presence. It can include things you intentionally share and data collected through your online activity.

Active footprintPosts, comments, likes, photos, videos and profiles you intentionally create.
Passive footprintInformation generated through browsing, app activity, cookies and other online interactions.

03 — COMMON RISKS

Know the risks. Spot them early.

01

Phishing & scams

Fake messages may try to make you reveal passwords, codes or personal information.

02

Fake profiles

Impersonation accounts can be used to deceive people or build trust for harmful purposes.

03

Oversharing

Publicly sharing your location, school, phone number or routines can create privacy risks.

04

Cyberbullying

Harmful online behavior can affect wellbeing. Save evidence, block and report when appropriate.

05

Account hacking

Weak or reused passwords can make accounts easier to compromise.

06

Reputation risks

Posts can be copied or shared. Consider how something may look later before publishing it.
